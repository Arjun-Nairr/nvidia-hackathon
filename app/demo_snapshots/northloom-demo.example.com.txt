== About ==
Northloom Demo Studio is a small, fictional studio that celebrates antique and traditional quilt patterns for the home.
We write about chintz prints, appliqued flowers, birds and baskets, and about how quilts were made by hand.
Our readers are people who love heritage patterns, collectors of traditional textiles and anyone choosing a thoughtful gift for a home.
Our voice is calm, factual and plain-spoken. We do not use hype, countdown timers or exaggerated claims.

== Collection ==
Featured piece: Chintz Applique Quilt
Printed and appliqued cotton, about 158.8 x 174 cm, made around 1820-30.
Held by The Metropolitan Museum of Art (object 2022.147.4); the photographs shown here are public-domain museum images.
The pattern shows a central basket of flowers, framed by floral borders with birds and flower sprays.
This is the only piece shown on the demo site.
