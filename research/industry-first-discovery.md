# Industry-first discovery

Updated 2026-10-09. Arjun clarified that even the delegated-specialist shortlist was too narrow. Current objective: identify industries where high-agency systems could own substantive work; Arjun will derive a project afterward. Do not convert an industry into a fixed project prematurely.

Selection judgment: favour industries with open-ended outcomes, many legitimate strategies, digital execution, frequent feedback and access to representative users/data. Provisional sectors: ecommerce/retail; marketing and creative agencies; travel/hospitality. This is not a quantified market ranking or proof of underserved demand.

## Ecommerce and retail

Potential responsibility: operate part of the merchant business toward margin, growth or customer-retention objectives. Agent can investigate the binding constraint and choose merchandising, promotions, supplier discussions, inventory actions or customer-resolution work. Outputs must be store/account changes and business follow-through, not advice. Available degrees of freedom and demonstration access are hypotheses to validate.

Current competitor fact: Shopify Sidekick advertises order/product tasks, third-party apps, background work and recommendations. Changes are presented for review. Shopify's June announcement is older than the July-October focus and is not the basis of a recent-launch claim. [Current documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/ai-powered-tools/sidekick).

Judgment: strongest balance of accessible test systems, meaningful actions and observable feedback. A generic store assistant would overlap Sidekick. Potential exploration: responsibility spanning merchant decisions and external counterparties, measured against existing software.

## Marketing and creative agencies

Potential responsibility: own a client campaign or production outcome from brief through strategy, creative work, distribution and iteration. Agent chooses hypotheses, creates and ships assets, coordinates collaborators, and adjusts based on observed response. The business user delegates an objective rather than a content calendar. Client communications and publishing require granted product authority, not approval of each ordinary step by default.

Recent fact: Adobe's August 11, 2026 article describes agents reaching across Adobe and third-party systems to perform work with business context. It establishes competitor direction, not measured autonomy or customer value. [Adobe](https://business.adobe.com/blog/human-agent-work-collaborators).

Judgment: strongest creative discretion and visible work; weakest causal measurement in a short demo. Don't equate more content with campaign success or pitch a generic post generator. Can explore campaign operations, creative production or client account work later.

## Travel and hospitality

Potential responsibility: represent a traveller, travel agency or accommodation operator across an ongoing journey/service outcome. Agent can choose and transact on options, negotiate with suppliers, coordinate people and handle changed circumstances. Broader than itinerary generation; supplier-side and traveller-side incentives differ, so choose whose behalf it acts on later.

Recent fact: Expedia announced its acquisition of Layla July 31, 2026, describing AI trip planning and booking strategy spanning flights, accommodation, activities and transport. This confirms substantial competitor activity, not a gap. [Expedia announcement](https://ir.expediagroup.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/Expedia-Group-acquires-Layla-accelerating-its-AI-powered-trip-planning-and-booking-strategy/default.aspx).

Judgment: strongest changing-world coordination, but hardest access to authentic booking/cancellation actions and reliable end-to-end demo. Evaluate actual transaction access before choosing a project. Generic trip planners are poor differentiation.

No industry or project selected. Discovery only. Prior narrow pitches are superseded; retain their research only as optional background. No installation, implementation, external messaging or reuse/publication of private projects authorized.
