8. Completed traces call flush ([plugin:668](C:/Users/dwish/AppData/Local/hermes/hermes-agent/plugins/observability/langfuse/__init__.py:668)). Session finalization flushes dangling traces and shutdown additionally closes its client ([plugin:967](C:/Users/dwish/AppData/Local/hermes/hermes-agent/plugins/observability/langfuse/__init__.py:967)). Use a guarded supported lifecycle callback at worker EXIT, not agent.close() on every message: closing an agent can mark its persistent session ended. Review bounded exporter/grace timeout; failures may still add latency or lose traces.
