## 1. Multiplayer indie-game playtest producer

Mandate example: get twenty suitable players into three useful playtests this week; improve participation and the next session based on what happens.

Agent freedom: choose audience and communities; decide whether to recruit through creators, existing game communities or playtest swaps; choose session format and incentives; produce recruitment assets and onboarding; initiate authorized outreach; coordinate real sessions; replace no-shows; investigate poor participation and change the next experiment. The agent may reject a broad recruitment campaign in favor of a smaller genre-specific cohort.

Output: real player participation, executed sessions, changed onboarding/recruitment assets and the next experiment already underway. It does not need to make the game or verify coding-agent completion.

Recent domain evidence: Valve's October 2026 Next Fest documentation lists September readiness milestones, October 8 press preview and October 19-26 festival. Registration closed August 31; a new hackathon project cannot assume eligibility to enter. This motivates demo/playtest work with an existing game, not a new-game launch deadline. [Valve event documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest/2026october?l=koreana&language=english).

Competition: Glitch's June 15 launch falls just outside the target recent window, so treat it as competitor background only. Its currently advertised agent covers launch/social/creator work and stops outreach/public posts for approval. PlayRecon displays a recorded July 26 case and markets game research and launch decisions. Neither page proves absence of playtest operations. [Glitch](https://www.glitch.fun/press), [PlayRecon](https://playrecon.com/).

Hypothesis: owning player turnout and useful multiplayer sessions is a stronger specialist responsibility than generic game marketing. Needs one actual playable game, a consenting Discord community and real players. Without that, a simulated marketing loop is weak.

Demo candidate: one existing permissioned student game, a separate Discord test server, human volunteers and real sessions. Inject a no-show and onboarding confusion; agent chooses and executes a response. Compare actual participation and interventions against manual organization. Do not claim commercial conversion from a small test.

## 2. Regional booking representative for unsigned performers

Mandate example: arrange a viable three-date weekend run that develops the audience and meets the band's commercial preferences.

Agent freedom: choose cities and venues; decide between support slots and headline shows; package the artist differently by audience; initiate authorized conversations; negotiate dates, fees and deal structures; evaluate whether one strategically valuable low-fee show improves the whole run; reroute after cancellation and reopen neighbouring discussions. User sets preferences, available dates and authority, not a sequence of venue lookups.

Output: counterpart-confirmed bookings, itinerary and operational arrangements. A sent pitch is not an agreed gig.

Recent primary context: September 14 WMC interview discusses city-level market choices, regional economics, support slots and smaller opportunities to develop demand. September 23 AFEM session addresses unsigned artists finding representation. These establish domain context, not quantified demand for an AI product. [WMC](https://wintermusicconference.com/2026/09/14/arcades-dave-alcock-on-turning-interest-into-tickets/), [AFEM](https://associationforelectronicmusic.org/2026/09/23/dj-producer-union-finding-an-agent/).

Current competitors: Music Mogul AI advertises career management including booking; LiveContact advertises venue selection/outreach; TourSmart covers routing, outreach and contracts. Their current pages are not evidence of launches in the last few months. No novelty claim. [Music Mogul AI](https://musicmogulai.com/), [LiveContact](https://livecontact.fr/en/agent-ia), [TourSmart](https://www.toursmart.io/).

Hypothesis: delegated pursuit and sustained negotiation across the run could be valuable to unsigned performers. Relationships and trust may dominate technical ability.

Demo candidate: an actual university band profile and consenting venue-role inboxes; public venue research; actual calendar/itinerary edits and varied counterpart replies. Label simulated counterpart negotiation clearly. Real gigs require real venue participation; the hackathon cannot demonstrate a tour happened within a short session.

## 3. Commercial manager for freelance 3D artists

Mandate example: build a pipeline of suitable game-asset commissions while preserving time for the artist's own portfolio work.

Agent freedom: decide what niche the portfolio supports; choose projects to pursue or reject; initiate authorized pitches; ask clients about ambiguous requirements; negotiate scope, price and revision tradeoffs; assemble relevant portfolio examples and artist-supplied previews; choose priorities across multiple clients; manage feedback and change requests; package and deliver approved artist outputs. It can propose a smaller paid pilot instead of accepting an underspecified large job. The human supplies artistic capability; the agent owns commercial follow-through.

Output: negotiated opportunities, organized client work, scheduled production commitments and delivery packages. It must not fabricate portfolio experience, promise unavailable capacity, or pretend a proposal is a sale.

Recent/current context: RenX task marketplace shows October 2026 listings and an agent-mediated interview/negotiate/execute/deliver model. This is a current first-party marketplace claim, not independent verification of task quality or a guarantee of revenue. Upwork's current official MCP page advertises proposals, messages and contract-related workflows; its write actions require confirmation, so do not assume it supports blanket autonomous sending. [RenX](https://renx.openmercury.com/tasks/), [Upwork MCP](https://www.upwork.com/ai/mcp).

Competition: generic freelance proposal agents already exist; current platforms supply agent-oriented work interfaces. We did not establish a gap for a 3D-specific commercial manager. Niche value would have to come from handling asset scope (format, polygon/texture budgets, style, engine, rights), portfolio fit, revisions and actual delivery alongside artist capacity. These are proposed requirements, not validated customer findings.

Demo candidate: one real artist's permissioned portfolio, three consenting prospective-client role inboxes with conflicting requirements, actual proposal/production workspace updates and one completed delivery from artist-supplied assets. Use direct consenting channels rather than claiming marketplace permission bypass. Evaluate deal quality and owner time saved, not number of messages. Need an artist collaborator before selecting this.
