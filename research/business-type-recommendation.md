# Business target recommendation — 2026-10-09

Discovery recommendation, not a selected project or implementation authorization.

## Judgment

Target an independent fashion/apparel retail brand that owns inventory and sells online. This builds on Arjun's reported textile advertising experience without assuming access to that company's systems, code, customer data, or publication rights. Manufacturing is a separate domain and is not implied by the word textile.

The proposed product direction is a merchant's commercial agent team. An owner delegates an outcome; agents investigate company and market circumstances, choose interventions, execute authorized changes, and revise strategy from results. Campaign generation is one possible action, not the whole product. Specialists can be created or reused when the work requires them, rather than following a compulsory agent roster.

Fashion offers a plausible combination of product visuals, seasonal demand, stock by size, margins, merchandising, customer questions, and campaign decisions. These are reasons to investigate this domain, not validated evidence of demand or superiority over existing products.

Illustrative demo hypothesis: asked to move an aging collection while maintaining margin, the agent notices the initially attractive product lacks commonly requested sizes, chooses a different collection or bundle, changes the store and campaign, then revises its approach when new customer or inventory evidence appears. Demonstrate decisions and completed changes. Simulated signals must be identified; they do not prove real sales uplift.
