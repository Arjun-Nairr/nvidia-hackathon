## Goal
One manual specialist-chat goal produces one saved flyer from US Facebook image-ad references and a persistent prepared home-textile product, with actual-image review and at most one corrective generation.

## Included
- Main/Laya provision the new semi-prebuilt social_media role with company-specific instructions/skills and backend-validated tools. Existing market analyst stays independent; no handoff or forced paired creation.
- Specialist chooses its own Facebook ad research, relevant visual references/features and supplied product photos. Ad data is distinct from Marketplace listings.
- Reported reach influences ranking only when verified available. Missing reach gates ranking, not generation; no proxy or invented metric.
- Persistent prepared sample: one product, 2–3 actual supplied photos, verified price/specifications and simple brand context. No upload interface.
- Gemini receives generated prompt/context plus selected merchant photos. Exact model/API/input/edit path is an explicit feasibility dependency.
- Backend integrity gate before actual-pixel agent review of product appearance, required text/price and layout. ONE initial generation plus AT MOST ONE corrective attempt, durably backend-enforced. Failed/unknown calls count; no automatic replay or third attempt.
- One final saved flyer with source/input/review provenance, retrievable after restart without inference. Unresolved defects explicitly flagged. Short explanation only if trivial.
- Minimal image display/status through replaceable API-driven UI. No dashboard redesign.

## Excluded
Analyst/social-agent coordination, Instagram, extra roles, schedules, posting/account writes, video, multiple final flyers, further corrections, fine-tuning, multi-business/auth/deployment, public repository work, private-project reuse and unrelated refactoring.

## Acceptance
- Main-to-social creation and existing analyst behavior work with separate tools, profiles, skills and memory.
- Facebook source and reach availability are honest; candidate references and merchant photos have recorded provenance and actual-pixel inspection.
- Gemini produces a valid stored image; actual review and bounded correction work or fail explicitly.
- Duplicate/concurrent/restart/provider-failure cases cannot bypass the two-generation limit; saved output/evidence retrieve without paid calls.
- Arjun judges one separately authorized, preserved live flyer; technical testing alone does not establish useful design.
- Relevant scoped tests pass; HANDOUT remains below400 lines. Full code review once per meaningful implementation ticket, corrections directly verified.
